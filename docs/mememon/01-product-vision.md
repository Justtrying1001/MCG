# 01 — Product Vision

## Vision statement
Mememon TCG turns internet-native culture into collectible cards with clear competitive utility and long-term community activation potential.

## Vision pillars
- **Collectible-first**: collection growth is the core user loop.
- **Edition-first scarcity direction**: editions are intended to be the primary player-facing collectible tier.
- **Weekly Tournament utility**: cards matter because they are used in recurring competitive lineups.
- **Community activation**: future partner/community formats extend utility beyond collection.
- **Staged commercialization**: internal demo reliability first, then broader monetization/trading scope.

## Runtime alignment note
Runtime currently still uses classic rarity in some systems (pack weighting, scoring, collection UI). Product direction is to phase that out of active player-facing design in favor of edition-first framing.

## Growth model by stage
| Stage | Vision focus |
|---|---|
| Internal Demo | Credible core loop demonstration for investors/internal stakeholders. |
| Alpha | Private tester reliability for packs -> collection -> Weekly Tournament utility. |
| Beta | Broader activation with improved economy controls and tournament operations. |
| V1 | First market launch with finalized commercial/trading scope. |
| Post-V1 | Advanced formats, partner/community activations, deeper web3 integrations. |

## Future-scoped systems (not current)
- Marketplace and secondary trading.
- Paid packs and real-money rails.
- Paid tournaments and prize pools.
- Mandatory on-chain ownership/trading enforcement.
