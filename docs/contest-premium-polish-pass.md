# Contest Premium Polish Pass (Ultra-short Audit)

## What was still too flat
- Team builder lacked emotional feedback while filling slots.
- Post-entry tracking existed but rank/score/phase impact was visually weak.
- Contest hub hierarchy between featured/open/live/settled was still too close.
- Rewards and urgency cues were present but not strongly emphasized.

## What this polish pass upgrades
- Premium lineup composition feel:
  - stronger slot visuals, completion progress bar, sticky action row, and quick eligible-card strip with selected/locked states.
- Stronger tracking state:
  - highlighted KPI row (rank/score/phase), improved timeline visibility, upgraded submitted-lineup and leaderboard emphasis.
- Hub tension and desirability:
  - spotlight hero strip + KPI overview + stronger featured contest card treatment.
- Enhanced gamification cues:
  - urgency/progression styling, podium chips, selected/locked state contrast, reward presentation improvements.
