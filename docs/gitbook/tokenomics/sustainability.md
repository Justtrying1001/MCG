# Sustainability

## Sustainability objective
MCG sustainability means balancing user incentives with controlled emissions and durable demand.

## Core sustainability levers
### Controlled issuance
Card and reward outputs should follow predefined limits and campaign logic.

### Sink-source balance
Resource outflows (pack costs, entry costs, marketplace fees) should offset reward outputs over time.

### Utility-driven demand
Demand should come from gameplay necessity and ecosystem participation, not temporary incentives alone.

## Risk areas to manage
- Excessive reward inflation without matching utility growth
- Reward concentration among a narrow user segment
- Weak onboarding that reduces new demand entering the system

## Sustainability checkpoints
A robust operating model should include regular reviews of:

- Participation trends
- Reward effectiveness
- Supply pressure from unlocks/emissions
- Long-term retention quality
