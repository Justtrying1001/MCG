# Bags Hackathon

## Why Bags.fm

MCG chose to launch through Bags.fm because the platform supports fair and transparent public launches without private sales.

It is also developer-oriented, which makes it a strong fit for builders shipping real products.

## The Bags Hackathon

Bags Hackathon provides a total of **$4,000,000** in builder support:

- **$1,000,000** distributed across 100 teams that ship products with real traction
- **$3,000,000** allocated to the Bags Fund to support projects with capital, distribution, and long-term acceleration

Source: [bags.fm](https://bags.fm/hackathon)

## Selection criteria and applications

Winning teams are selected based on real traction indicators, including:

- On-chain performance
- Product usage
- Growth potential and trajectory

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, and growth momentum is a key signal in selection.
