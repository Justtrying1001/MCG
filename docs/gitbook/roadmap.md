# Roadmap

## Roadmap purpose
This roadmap outlines execution priorities rather than fixed promises. Delivery should follow product readiness and security constraints.

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Stabilize pack and tournament core loops
- Improve onboarding clarity and player UX
- Strengthen reward ledger transparency

## Phase 2 — Competitive depth
- Expand tournament format variety
- Improve lineup tooling and score explainability
- Introduce deeper progression milestones

## Phase 3 — Ecosystem expansion
- Expand marketplace capabilities
- Launch stronger community campaign infrastructure
- Add broader partner integrations

## Phase 4 — Governance maturity
- Introduce scoped governance processes
- Publish decision and treasury reporting standards
- Expand community influence on ecosystem priorities

## Ongoing priorities
- Security and reliability
- Economic monitoring
- Documentation updates as systems evolve
