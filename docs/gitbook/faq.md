# FAQ

## 1) How do I start playing MCG?
Create or connect your account, check your starting balance/resources, open your first pack, then enter an `OPEN` tournament with eligible cards.

## 2) How many cards are in a pack?
Each pack contains **5 cards**.

## 3) Can I use the same card in multiple tournaments at once?
Not always. If a card is locked by an active tournament state, it is temporarily unavailable for other entries.

## 4) How are tournament rewards calculated?
Rewards are determined by tournament settlement logic, ranking outcomes, and configured reward plans. Scoring uses runtime data and applicable multipliers.

## 5) What should I do if rewards do not appear immediately?
Check tournament status first. Rewards are generally distributed after settlement and may require a claim step depending on reward type.

## 6) Is MCG only for experienced Web3 users?
No. New users can follow the onboarding flow, start with basic pack openings, and learn through smaller tournament participation.

## 7) What is the difference between card rarity and card utility?
Rarity indicates scarcity profile. Utility indicates how useful a card is under current tournament rules and market demand.

## 8) Why is token sustainability important?
Sustainability protects long-term player incentives by keeping emissions, utility demand, and participation economics balanced.

## 9) Is governance already fully decentralized?
Typically no in early phases. Governance is often introduced progressively, starting with limited scope while core operations remain safeguarded.

## 10) Where should I look for official updates?
Use official MCG channels and this documentation. Major rule or economy changes should be reflected in updated docs and announcements.
