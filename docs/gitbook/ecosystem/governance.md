# Governance

## Current relevance
Governance is relevant as MCG evolves beyond a centrally managed Genesis phase.

## Governance scope (progressive)
Potential governance areas include:

- Tournament format priorities
- Reward policy adjustments
- Ecosystem budget allocation principles
- Community initiative support

## Governance design principles
- Start with clearly scoped, low-risk decisions.
- Publish rationale and expected impact for proposals.
- Maintain operational safeguards for security-critical systems.

## Practical expectation
In early stages, governance may be limited or advisory. Decision power can expand as tooling, participation quality, and operational readiness improve.
