# Community

## Community role in MCG
Community is a core operating layer for education, competitive coordination, and feedback loops.

## Typical community functions
### Player education
Guides, lineup discussions, and tournament analysis reduce onboarding friction.

### Event activation
Community campaigns and social missions can increase engagement and cross-ecosystem visibility.

### Feedback channel
Structured community feedback helps improve gameplay balance and reward design.

## Healthy community indicators
- Consistent onboarding support for new players
- High quality discussions around strategy and fairness
- Constructive reporting of issues and improvements
