# Progression System

## Objective of progression
The progression system provides a structured path from onboarding to competitive consistency.

## Progression components
### Collection growth
Acquire enough playable cards to avoid dependence on a single lineup pattern.

### Tournament consistency
Sustained placement performance typically matters more than isolated wins.

### Quest completion
Quests can accelerate progression by adding supplementary rewards and milestones.

## Suggested player stages
1. **Starter stage**: open initial packs, learn eligibility, enter low-risk tournaments.
2. **Builder stage**: expand coverage and test lineup strategies across formats.
3. **Competitive stage**: optimize entry timing, lock management, and reward efficiency.

## What strong progression looks like
- Predictable participation cadence
- Positive long-term reward efficiency
- Better lineup quality from deeper collection intelligence
