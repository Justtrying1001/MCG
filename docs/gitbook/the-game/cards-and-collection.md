# Cards & Collection

## Card model
Each card belongs to a project/token and includes metadata that can influence utility:

- Rarity tier
- Edition characteristics
- Ownership state
- Eligibility status for current tournaments

## Collection management
The collection page is your operational inventory. It should be used to:

1. Review which cards are free versus locked.
2. Identify high-impact cards for upcoming tournaments.
3. Track depth by token/project.

## Card lock behavior
When a card is committed to certain tournament stages, it can become temporarily locked.

During lock period:

- It cannot be moved to another lineup.
- It remains in your inventory but is not re-deployable.

## Strategic implications
- Broad collections improve flexibility across tournament formats.
- Concentrated collections can deliver stronger specialization if format rules favor specific profiles.
