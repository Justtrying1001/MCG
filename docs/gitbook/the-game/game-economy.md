# Game Economy

## Economy overview
MCG economy connects acquisition, competition, and rewards into one loop:

1. Spend resources to open packs and build collection.
2. Deploy cards into tournaments.
3. Receive rewards from performance and activities.
4. Reinvest based on strategy.

## Genesis supply framework
Current planned supply parameters:

- 80,000 total cards
- 16,000 pack capacity at 5 cards per pack

This creates a controlled relationship between card issuance and pack throughput.

## Main sinks and sources
### Sinks
- Pack opening costs
- Tournament entry costs (where enabled)

### Sources
- Tournament rewards
- Quest and campaign rewards

## Economic health indicators
Players and investors should monitor:

- Participation growth across tournaments
- Reward concentration versus broad distribution
- Pack issuance pace relative to active demand
- Net utility of cards after tournament cycles
