# Rewards

## Reward categories
MCG rewards can include:

- Points
- Pack rewards
- Manual or campaign-specific rewards

## Reward sources
### Tournament settlement
Most rewards are distributed after `SETTLED` status according to ranking plans.

### Quests and campaigns
Additional rewards can be earned via quests, missions, or special events.

## Claim and tracking
Users should monitor:

- Reward history in ledger endpoints/pages
- Tournament-specific reward views (for event-linked earnings)
- Claim actions where required by reward type

## Practical recommendations
- Reconcile your ledger regularly to track net gain versus entry spend.
- Prioritize tournaments where your collection has strong eligibility depth.
