# What is MCG

## Product definition
MCG is a Web3 collectible competitive game where players:

1. Acquire packs.
2. Build a card collection.
3. Enter tournaments with selected lineups.
4. Earn rewards based on performance.

## Core game pillars
### Collect
Cards represent exposure to in-game projects/tokens and come with rarity and edition attributes.

### Compete
Players submit eligible cards into tournament formats with defined start, lock, and settlement phases.

### Earn
Rewards are distributed based on ranking, participation rules, and active campaign configuration.

## Supply baseline
Current Genesis baseline used by MCG systems:

- **25 active projects/tokens**
- **3,200 cards per token**
- **80,000 planned cards total**
- **5 cards per pack**
- **16,000 planned packs capacity**

## Practical outcome for users
- Players can progress by combining collection depth and tournament skill.
- Economic value is influenced by card utility, scarcity, and tournament demand.
