# What is MCG?

MCG is a **crypto trading card game** where the cards are real meme tokens and the contests are live market battles.

---

## The core idea

In traditional trading card games, you collect cards and compete based on fixed stats. In MCG, the stats are **live**. A card's performance in a contest depends on how its underlying token actually moves in the market — price, volume, market cap, and momentum all factor in.

You're not just a collector. You're a market strategist.

---

## The game loop

1. **Sign in** with your Twitter (X) account
2. **Open packs** to collect meme token cards
3. **Build a collection** across different tokens, rarities, and editions
4. **Enter contests** by assembling a roster from your cards
5. **Watch the market** — token performance during the contest window drives your score
6. **Earn rewards** in points based on your score
7. **Progress** through quests and milestones as you play
8. **Repeat** — new packs, new contests, new opportunities

---

## Why meme tokens?

Meme tokens are the most culturally alive corner of crypto. They move fast, they generate energy, and they have dedicated communities. That volatility — usually seen as a drawback — becomes the engine of MCG gameplay.

The chaos is the game.
