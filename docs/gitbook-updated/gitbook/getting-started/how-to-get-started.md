# How to Get Started

Getting into MCG takes less than five minutes. Here's how.

---

## Step 1 — Sign in

Visit MCG and sign in via Privy. The web modal prioritizes **Solana wallet** login, with **Twitter (X)** also available. On mobile browser, wallet auth is supported through wallet app deep-linking and WalletConnect fallback.

---

## Step 2 — Get your Welcome Reward

New players receive a **Welcome Reward** on sign-up: **500 bonus points** added to your account automatically.

That's enough to open your first pack right away.

---

## Step 3 — Open your first pack

Head to the Pack Store. Each pack costs **500 points** and contains **5 cards**.

Your first pack is the beginning of your collection. Every card you pull can be used in contests or kept as a collectible.

See [Packs & Opening](../cards-and-packs/packs-and-opening.md) for details on how pack opening works.

---

## Step 4 — Explore your collection

After opening, visit your Collection to see what you've pulled. Each card shows its token, rarity, edition, and visual style.

---

## Step 5 — Enter a contest

Once you have cards, you can enter a **Contest**. Select an open contest, assemble your roster from your collection, and lock it in before the contest starts.

From there, the market does the work. See [How Contests Work](../contests/how-contests-work.md).

---

## Step 6 — Earn, progress, repeat

After a contest settles, your points are credited. Use them to open more packs, and work through [Quests & Milestones](../progression/quests-and-milestones.md) to unlock bonus rewards as you go.

---

## Tips for new players

- Complete your early milestones — they reward a lot of points for simple actions
- Rarer cards don't always win contests; a well-rounded roster matters
- Check the contest lock time before building your roster — once locked, no changes are possible
