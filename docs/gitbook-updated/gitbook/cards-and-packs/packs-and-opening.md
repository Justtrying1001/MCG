# Packs & Opening

Packs are how you build your collection. Each pack contains 5 cards drawn from the current set.

---

## Types of packs

### Sale Pack

Available for purchase in the Pack Store.

- **Cost:** 500 points
- **Contents:** 5 cards
- **Total supply:** 10,000 packs (50,000 cards)
- **Pack code:** `genesis_sale_pack`

### Reward Pack

Earned through gameplay — contests, milestones, and special events.

- **Cost:** Free (earned)
- **Contents:** 5 cards
- **Total supply:** 6,000 packs (30,000 cards)
- **Pack code:** `genesis_reward_pack`

## How pack opening works

Each pack contains **5 card slots**. Each slot draws independently, with different odds depending on the slot position.

- **Slots 1–3** draw with standard odds
- **Slot 4** has an enhanced chance of hitting rarer editions (HOLO, FULL_ART)
- **Slot 5** is the "rarity hit" slot — significantly elevated odds for RARE, EPIC, and LEGENDARY

This means every pack has a guaranteed shot at something notable in the last slot, while slots 1–3 build out your core collection.

See [Drop Rates & Odds](drop-rates-and-odds.md) for full odds breakdown.

---

## Pack supply is finite

There are no reprints. Once the 16,000 packs of Set 1 are opened, they're gone. Cards will continue to circulate through player collections, but new supply from packs will be exhausted.
