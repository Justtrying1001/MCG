# Drop Rates & Odds

Each pack slot draws cards independently. The odds vary by slot — some are standard, others are weighted toward rarer outcomes.

---

## How the draw system works

When you open a pack, each of the 5 slots runs its own draw. The draw considers:

- **Remaining supply** of each card — as cards are pulled from packs globally, rarer cards with low remaining supply become harder to find naturally
- **Slot type** — different slots have different multipliers that boost the chance of hitting certain rarities or editions

This means odds are **dynamic**. Early in the game's life, when supply is high, the distribution is closer to baseline. As packs are opened over time, the remaining pool shifts.

---

## Slot types

| Slot | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standard | Baseline odds across all rarities |
| 2 | Standard | Baseline odds across all rarities |
| 3 | Standard | Baseline odds across all rarities |
| 4 | Edition Boost | Elevated chance of hitting non-BASE editions |
| 5 | Rarity Hit | Significantly elevated chance of RARE, EPIC, or LEGENDARY |

---

## Approximate odds by slot

These are indicative ranges based on current supply conditions. Actual odds shift as global supply changes.

**Slots 1–3 (Standard)**

| Rarity | Approx. chance |
|---|---|
| COMMON | ~73% |
| UNCOMMON | ~19% |
| RARE | ~6% |
| EPIC | ~1.5% |
| LEGENDARY | ~0.3% |

**Slot 4 (Edition Boost)**

Similar rarity distribution to standard slots, but the draw pool is heavily weighted toward non-BASE editions — your best chance at HOLO and FULL_ART cards.

**Slot 5 (Rarity Hit)**

| Rarity | Approx. chance |
|---|---|
| COMMON | ~10% |
| UNCOMMON | ~12% |
| RARE | ~27% |
| EPIC | ~28% |
| LEGENDARY | ~23% |

> ⚠️ These figures are estimates based on initial supply conditions. They shift as more packs are opened globally. Treat them as directional, not exact.

---

## What this means in practice

- Every pack has one slot (Slot 5) with dramatically elevated odds for rare pulls
- Slot 4 is your best chance at hitting special editions (HOLO, FULL_ART)
- The longer the game runs and the more packs are opened, the scarcer high-rarity cards become in the remaining pool
