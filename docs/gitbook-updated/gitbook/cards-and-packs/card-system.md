# Card System

Every card in MCG represents a real meme token. Cards are collectible, playable, and come in a range of rarities and editions that affect both their appearance and their in-game multipliers.

---

## Card anatomy

Each card has:

- **Token** — the meme coin it represents (e.g. DOGE, PEPE, WIF)
- **Rarity** — how rare the card is within its token's supply
- **Edition** — the visual and mechanical variant of the card

---

## Rarities

There are five rarity tiers, from most common to rarest:

| Rarity | Description |
|---|---|
| **COMMON** | The backbone of any collection. High supply, widely available. |
| **UNCOMMON** | A step up. More distinct visuals, slightly lower supply. |
| **RARE** | Noticeably harder to pull. Strong contest multiplier. |
| **EPIC** | Scarce and impactful. Sought-after in competitive rosters. |
| **LEGENDARY** | The rarest cards in the game. Powerful multipliers, very limited supply. |

Rarity affects **card score multipliers** in contests. Rarer cards amplify your score further.

---

## Editions

Each rarity can appear in multiple editions. Editions affect both the visual style of the card and add an additional multiplier on top of rarity.

| Edition | Description |
|---|---|
| **BASE** | The standard card. Clean and classic. |
| **REVERSE** | An alternate colorway with a distinct feel. |
| **BRILLANTE** | Shimmering finish. More visually striking. |
| **HOLO** | Full holographic treatment. A standout in any collection. |
| **FULL_ART** | The rarest edition — unique illustrated artwork. Maximum edition multiplier. |

The combination of rarity + edition determines a card's full multiplier in contests.

---

## Supply

Each token has a fixed total supply of **3,200 cards** distributed across all rarity and edition combinations. Supply is set at the time of set creation and does not change.

The rarest edition of the rarest rarity is genuinely scarce — there are very few of them in existence.

---

## Card sets

Cards are released in **Sets**. The current set is **Set 1 — GENESIS** (code: `GENESIS_SET_V1`), featuring 25 meme token cards.

Future sets will expand the card pool with new tokens and new mechanics.
