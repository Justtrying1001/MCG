## What the token is for

The long-term vision is simple: **the MCG token becomes the currency of MCG.**

Right now, the game runs on an off-chain points system. Over time, token integration will progressively connect in-game activity to on-chain value.

This integration happens in phases:

- **Phase 1** — Bonus packs and points rewards for $MCG holders
- **Phase 2** — Holder-only features and advantages
- **Phase 3** — Pack purchases and contest entry denominated in $MCG
- **Phase 4** — Holder-exclusive tournament formats

The exact shape of each phase will be communicated as it ships. We don't overpromise specifics — we ship them.

---

## Why a token?

MCG sits at the intersection of crypto culture and gaming. A native token creates a real economic layer around the game, aligns player incentives, and gives the community skin in the game.

Meme token culture is already baked into MCG's DNA. The $MCG token is the project making that relationship explicit.
