# Rewards

After a contest settles, rewards are distributed based on final rankings.

---

## How rewards work

Every contest has a defined **reward pool** distributed across finishing positions. The higher your final ranking, the larger your share.

Rewards are paid in **points**, which can be used to open more packs, participate in future contests, and progress through milestones.

---

## Reward types

Contests can award different types of rewards depending on how they're configured:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Points** | Credited directly to your balance |
| **Reward Packs** | Free packs added to your inventory |

---

## Reward Packs

Some contests award **Reward Packs** directly — free packs that go straight to your inventory. These are separate from the Sale Pack supply and represent one of the primary ways to grow your collection without spending points directly.

---

## Settlement

Rewards are credited automatically after settlement. No action required on your part — once the contest closes and scores are finalized, your account is updated.

---

## No entry, no reward

If a contest is **CANCELED** (due to insufficient participation), any entry costs are returned and no rewards are distributed.

---

## Points earned from rewards

Rewards stack with your existing points balance and count toward [milestone](../progression/quests-and-milestones.md) progression — every point earned from contests moves you forward.
