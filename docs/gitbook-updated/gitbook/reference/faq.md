# FAQ

Answers to common questions about MCG.

---

**Do I need a wallet to play?**  
No. MCG uses Twitter (X) OAuth for authentication via Privy. No wallet connection is required to collect cards, enter contests, or earn rewards.

---

**What happens if I miss the roster lock?**  
If you haven't submitted an entry before a contest moves to LOCKED, you cannot participate in that contest. Make sure to submit your roster while the contest is still in the OPEN phase.

---

**Do the drop odds change over time?**  
Yes. The draw system accounts for remaining supply across all packs globally. As more packs are opened and high-rarity cards become scarcer in the pool, the odds of pulling them naturally shift. Odds listed in this doc are estimates based on initial supply conditions.

---

**Can I earn points without opening packs?**  
Yes. Contest rewards, milestone completions, and quest rewards all generate points independently. You don't need to spend on packs to earn points — though packs are the main way to build the collection you need to compete.

---

**What is a FULL_ART card?**  
FULL_ART is the rarest edition. These cards feature unique illustrated artwork specific to each token. They carry the highest edition multiplier and are among the most sought-after cards in the game. Only 2 LEGENDARY FULL_ART copies exist per token.

---

**Can I trade or sell my cards?**  
Trading and a secondary market are on the roadmap. This feature is not yet available in the current MVP. Stay tuned for updates.

---

**What is the MCG token and where can I get it?**  
The MCG token ($MCG) is the native token of the MCG ecosystem, launched on Bags. See [The MCG Token](../tokenomics/mcg-token.md) for full details including the contract address.

---

**How are ties handled in contests?**  
Ties are resolved in order: (1) sum of raw token scores, (2) number of tokens that scored above 50, (3) best single token score, (4) user ID as last resort.

---

**What does a token score of 50 mean?**  
50 is the neutral score — it means the token's performance was flat, or market data was unavailable for that signal. Above 50 is positive performance; below 50 is negative.

---

**Will there be more card sets after GENESIS?**  
Yes. Set 1 — GENESIS is the first of multiple planned sets. New sets will introduce new tokens and potentially new mechanics.

---

**Where can I follow MCG updates?**  
Follow MCG on Twitter and check this documentation — it's updated as new features ship.
