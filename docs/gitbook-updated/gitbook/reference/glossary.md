# Glossary

Key terms used throughout MCG and this documentation.

---

**Card Template**  
The base definition of a card — its token, rarity, and edition. The template is what's designed; instances are what players own.

**Card Score**  
A card's final score in a contest, calculated by applying rarity and edition multipliers to the underlying token score.

**Contest Entry**  
A player's registered roster for a specific contest. Entries are locked before the market window begins.

**Edition**  
The visual and mechanical variant of a card. Editions (BASE, REVERSE, BRILLANTE, HOLO, FULL_ART) each carry a score multiplier.

**FULL_ART**  
The rarest edition in the game. Features unique illustrated artwork and carries the highest edition multiplier.

**GENESIS_SET_V1**  
The code for the first MCG card set, covering 25 meme tokens.

**Milestone**  
A permanent account achievement that rewards points (and sometimes packs) when reached. Milestones are completed once and do not reset.

**Owned Card Instance**  
A specific card in a player's collection — a unique copy of a card template, owned by one account.

**Pack**  
A sealed set of 5 cards. Packs are opened to add cards to your collection.

**Rarity**  
The scarcity tier of a card (COMMON, UNCOMMON, RARE, EPIC, LEGENDARY). Rarity affects supply and score multipliers.

**Reward Ledger**  
The internal record of all point transactions on an account, including contest results, milestones, quests, and pack purchases.

**Roster Lock**  
The moment a contest moves to LOCKED phase. After this point, no changes to contest entries are possible.

**Settlement**  
The process of finalizing a contest — calculating scores, determining rankings, and distributing rewards.

**Snapshot**  
A capture of token market data (price, volume, market cap, rank) at a specific moment. Contests use start and end snapshots to measure performance.

**Token Score**  
The market performance score assigned to a token during a contest window, derived from price, volume, market cap, and rank signals.
