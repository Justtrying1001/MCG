# Points & Economy

Points are the primary currency of MCG. You earn them, spend them, and use them to track your progress.

---

## How you earn points

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| Welcome Reward (new accounts) | +500 pts |
| Contest rewards | Varies by ranking |
| Milestone completion | Varies by milestone (100–25,000 pts) |
| Quest completion | Varies by quest |

New players receive **500 points** as a welcome reward upon first sign-in — enough to open one pack immediately.

---

## How you spend points

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Sale Pack | 500 pts |
| Contest entry (where applicable) | Varies by contest |

---

## The economy loop

Points flow in a consistent loop:

1. Receive welcome reward on sign-up
2. Open packs → build collection
3. Enter contests → earn more points from rewards
4. Use points to open more packs
5. Complete milestones → earn bonus points along the way

The more you play, the more points you generate — contests are the primary engine for sustained point generation beyond your starting balance.

---

## Points are not transferable

Points are account-bound. They cannot be traded, gifted, or transferred between players. They exist purely as an in-game progression currency.
