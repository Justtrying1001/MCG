# Quests & Milestones

MCG rewards players for playing, exploring, and growing. Quests and milestones are the progression system that tracks this journey.

---

## Milestones

Milestones are permanent, one-time achievements tied to your account. Each one is reached once and rewards you with points (and sometimes packs) when you hit it.

| Pack veteran | 20 packs | 2,000 pts |
| Pack master | 50 packs | 5,000 pts |
| Pack legend | 200 packs | 15,000 pts |

## Quests

Quests are objectives that reward engagement with specific parts of the game.

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Welcome** | One-time quest rewarded on first sign-in |
| **Social** | Follow MCG on X (Twitter) |
| **Contest milestones** | Auto-tracked based on contest activity |
| **Manual** | Submitted by the player, reviewed by the team |

Quest statuses progress from Available → In Progress → Claimable → Completed.

Check the quest board in-game for what's currently active.

---

## Why it matters

Every milestone and quest reward goes directly into your points balance — which means more packs, more cards, and more contests. Staying on top of milestones is one of the most efficient ways to grow your collection without spending externally.
